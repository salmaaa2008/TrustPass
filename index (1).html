How it works

01

Issue

Universities, bootcamps and employers issue credentials. Only a fingerprint is anchored on Hedera, never personal data.

02

Own

Students keep their credentials and share only what is needed.

03

Verify

A recruiter’s AI agent reads a CV and checks each claim against the proof. The AI proposes, the cryptography decides.

Verify a document

Live. This page checks documents on Hedera testnet, in your browser. A TrustPass document carries a QR code that holds the credential. The page recomputes its fingerprint and looks for it in the issuer’s registry.
